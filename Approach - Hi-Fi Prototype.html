Approach

Disciplined execution across the ownership cycle.

Responsibilities

Investor retains

  • Investment objectives
  • Capital commitment
  • Ownership
  • Investment decisions
  • Final approvals

Reiwa Capital coordinates

  • Market sourcing
  • Opportunity assessment
  • Structuring and financing
  • Due diligence and acquisition
  • Reporting and asset oversight

Investment decisions and ownership remain with the investor.

The Process
01

Mandate Definition

Establish the investor’s objectives and parameters before opportunities are pursued.

Hold period · Return objectives · Risk · Financing · Reporting

+Detail

Each mandate begins with the investor’s objectives: hold period, return objectives, risk tolerance, financing parameters, tax, foreign exchange and reporting requirements. This defines what to pursue, what to avoid and how each opportunity is assessed.

02

Sourcing and Underwriting

Opportunities are sourced through local relationships, direct owner contact and adviser networks.

Location · Income quality · Condition · Long-term fit

+Detail

Each opportunity is reviewed for location, income quality, condition, ownership structure and long‑term fit.

03

Structuring

Each holding is structured around the investor’s requirements.

Ownership · Financing · Reporting · Exit

+Detail

Legal, tax and accounting advisers are engaged so ownership, financing, reporting and exit considerations are addressed before acquisition.

04

Acquisition Execution

Reiwa Capital coordinates sellers, advisers, lenders, consultants and operating partners through completion.

Counterparties · Sequence · Completion

+Detail

Cross-border transactions require disciplined coordination. Decisions stay clear and in sequence.

05

Asset Oversight

Oversight continues through reporting, business planning, leasing, capital projects, refinancing and exit.

Reporting · Leasing · Capital projects · Exit

+Detail

Ownership begins at completion.

Reiwa Capital supports investors who want direct ownership in Europe without running every local workstream themselves.
