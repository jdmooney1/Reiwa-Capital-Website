Areas of focus

Income, Repositioning, Hospitality.

01

Income

Assets supported by established demand and a visible, defensible income profile.

Asset types
Offices, prime retail and residential supported by established occupier demand.
Considerations
Covenant strength, lease profile, rental sustainability and reletting prospects.
02

Repositioning

Assets with identifiable potential to improve occupancy, income and long-term value.

Asset types
Office and mixed-use buildings with vacancy, underused space or a dated leasing proposition.
Considerations
Refurbishment scope and cost, planning position, programme, achievable rent and stabilisation.
03

Hospitality

Hotels and serviced accommodation where location, trading performance and asset quality support long-term value.

Asset types
Branded and independent hotels, aparthotels, serviced apartments and extended-stay accommodation.
Considerations
Trading performance, operator and brand strength, lease or operating structure, capital requirements and exit liquidity.
