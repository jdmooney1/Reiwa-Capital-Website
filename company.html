An Investor-Side Platform.

Reiwa Capital works from the investor's side of the table. Acquiring an asset abroad is only part of the investment; objectives set at the outset need to survive underwriting, execution and years of ownership, through changes of adviser, counterparty and market.

The platform concentrates on London and Amsterdam rather than broad geographic coverage, and on a small number of strategies within them, so that judgement is built where it can be applied repeatedly.

Specialist work is carried out by appointed European advisers. Reiwa Capital coordinates them against the mandate, and investment authority stays with the investor throughout.

Scope of service

Clear About What Sits Where.

Reiwa Capital provides investor-side advisory and ownership oversight. It does not provide legal, tax, valuation or property-management advice.

Those services are provided by specialist advisers appointed by, and accountable to, the investor or the ownership vehicle. Reiwa Capital coordinates their work against the agreed mandate; material decisions remain subject to investor approval.
