Company

Reiwa Capital is an independent, investor-side adviser on UK and Dutch real estate for Japanese investors. It operates from Tokyo with execution in London and Amsterdam.

Entity

Principal office
Tokyo
Markets
London, Amsterdam

Independence

Reiwa Capital acts for the investor only, under a mandate agreed with the investor.

What Reiwa Capital Does Not Do

  • Does not hold or manage client money.

  • Does not offer, promote or manage collective investment products.

  • Does not provide legal, tax or valuation advice. These are carried out by separately appointed, regulated specialists.

How Engagements Are Structured

Each engagement begins with a written mandate agreed with the investor, setting out objectives, markets, return requirements, constraints and the decisions reserved to the investor. Reiwa Capital works alongside the investor’s existing advisers and reports in Japanese and English.
