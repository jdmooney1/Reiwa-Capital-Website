Content removed

  • Approach: the separate kicker+H1 “Approach” pairing and its own opening statement are merged into one dominant headline (kicker demoted to a small label). The old two-sentence paragraph per stage is cut to its first, already-approved sentence; the second sentence's substance is compressed into a three-word considerations line.
  • Investment Focus: the entire “Investment Strategy” intro block is removed — it duplicated the page's own subhead (“Three strategies across London and Amsterdam.” appeared twice) and the five-filter grid (Location Quality / Income Profile / Asset Relevance / Execution Requirement / Exit Liquidity) is removed as generic, not tied to any image or chapter. No claim from either is lost: filters like income quality and exit liquidity are implicit in each chapter's own thesis and underwriting field.

No new investment claims

Per correction #6, nothing was added to the strategy copy. Each chapter's thesis is the live site's fg-say sentence, unchanged, character for character. The wireframe's earlier second sentences (“Covenant strength and lease term matter more than yield alone,” “Refurbishment and planning risk are priced in, not assumed away,” “Operator covenant matters as much as the real estate”) do not appear in the prototype and were struck from the wireframe too. The illustrative three-word criteria tags in the wireframe remain marked as placeholders pending copy approval — the prototype itself does not use them at all, to stay strictly inside approved copy.

Content placed behind disclosure

  • Investment Focus — each chapter's Asset Types and Underwriting fields (existing approved copy, unchanged) move behind a native <details>/<summary> labelled “What we look for”. Open by default in print and with JavaScript disabled — nothing factual is ever hidden from a diligence reader working from a PDF or a text browser.
  • Approach — no content is hidden; the spine shows every stage's objective sentence and considerations line at all times. The only disclosure-like behaviour is the diagram's one-time entrance fade, which resolves to the fully visible end-state within 500ms (and instantly under prefers-reduced-motion).

Interaction notes

  • Operating-model diagram (Approach) — static, no click targets. Language is carried entirely by the real EN/JA header toggle, exactly like the rest of the site; the diagram never shows both languages at once. Entrance is a quiet three-step opacity fade (≤500ms), reusing the same IntersectionObserver pattern already shipped in production, retargeted to the new markup.
  • Expanders (Investment Focus) — native <details>, so click/tap, Enter and Space all work with no custom script; state is per-chapter and independent. The “+” glyph rotates 45° to read as “×” when open (≤280ms, the site's standard hover/toggle duration).
  • Navigation, footer, language toggle, drawer — unchanged: both prototypes load the real shell.js/i18n.js/craft.js and the production stylesheets, so header state, the drawer, next-chapter routing and the approved footer are the live behaviour, not a mockup.

Accessibility behaviour

  • Both pages keep one <h1> and a logical heading order (stage and chapter titles are <h2>).
  • <details>/<summary> is natively keyboard- and screen-reader-operable (exposes expanded/collapsed state without ARIA wiring); the marker is decorative and duplicated by the visible label text, not the only cue.
  • The op-model connector labels (“Advisory mandate”, “Local coordination”) are real text, not baked into the arrow SVGs (which carry aria-hidden), so they read correctly in sequence for assistive tech.
  • Reduced-motion: the diagram's entrance fade and the expander icon rotation both respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant, no transition).
  • Colour and type are untouched from the approved system — no new contrast risk introduced.

What was not touched

Home, About, Contact, Privacy and all production files (index.html, about.html, contact.html, privacy.html, editorial.css, shell.js, etc.) are unchanged. The two files above are net-new, self-contained prototypes for review; nothing links to them from the live site.
