Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Reiwa Capital handles personal information received through this website and direct enquiries.

Last updated: August 2026

01Information received

This website does not use an online enquiry form. If a visitor contacts Reiwa Capital by email, the information received may include their name, email address and any details provided in the message.

The website stores the selected language preference locally in the visitor’s browser. This preference is not used for advertising or behavioural tracking.

The website’s hosting provider may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and access logs, for security and service operation.

02How information is used

Personal information is used to respond to enquiries and, where a professional relationship develops, to administer and communicate about that relationship.

Information may also be processed where necessary to maintain website security, comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate business interests. Enquiry information is not used for marketing without consent.

03Sharing and international processing

Information may be shared with professional advisers, service providers or public authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required. Reiwa Capital does not sell personal information.

Some service providers may process information outside the country in which it was collected. Where applicable, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

04Retention and security

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was received, including legal, regulatory and record-keeping requirements.

Reasonable organisational and technical measures are used to protect information. No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05Individual rights

Depending on the applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability.

Requests may be sent to info@reiwa-capital.com. Identity verification may be required before a request is completed. Individuals may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

06Cookies and contact

This website does not currently use non-essential advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. If analytics or other tracking technologies are introduced, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated before deployment.
