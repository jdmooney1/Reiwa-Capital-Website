Approach

From investment mandate through acquisition and ownership.

Process

Mandate definition

Before opportunities are considered, the investor’s objectives are established: hold period, return requirements, risk parameters, financing, tax, foreign exchange and reporting.

These parameters determine what is pursued and how each opportunity is assessed.

Sourcing and assessment

Opportunities are sourced through local relationships, direct owner contact and adviser networks. Each asset is assessed on its location, income, condition, price and long-term suitability.

The investment case is built at asset level.

Ownership and financing

Ownership, financing, tax, foreign exchange and reporting are considered together before an acquisition proceeds.

Acquisition

Reiwa Capital coordinates due diligence, negotiation, financing and completion across sellers, lenders and professional advisers.

The investor retains approval at each material decision point.

Ownership oversight

Following completion, Reiwa Capital coordinates reporting, business planning, leasing, capital works, refinancing and eventual exit against the agreed ownership plan.

Oversight continues throughout the investment period.

Decisions retained by the investor

  • Investment mandate
  • Capital commitment
  • Asset approval
  • Hold, refinance and exit decisions

Work coordinated by Reiwa Capital

  • Sourcing and assessment
  • Ownership and financing
  • Acquisition execution
  • Reporting and local asset workstreams
