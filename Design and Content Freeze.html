Source-of-truth record of the approved Reiwa Capital website as built, 31 July 2026. Governs all subsequent audits. Anything described in sections A and B is settled and must not be redesigned; sections C and D are the only open surface.

Frozen · v1.0 · no changes made

1 · Positioning

Reiwa Capital is an investor-side European real estate investment firm. It supports investors assessing and executing European real estate investments; coordinates local advisers, financing, acquisition and ownership workstreams; works across London and Amsterdam; supports income, repositioning and hospitality strategies. It does not exercise discretionary investment authority unless expressly stated, does not replace appointed legal, tax, technical or valuation advisers, and must never be presented as a property manager, broker or generic consultant.

3 · Navigation

  • Fixed bar: lockup left; EN / JA toggle and menu button right.
  • Drawer overlay: five page links, email, LinkedIn. Focus trap, Escape to close, scrim dismiss, focus returned on close.
  • Onward flow, one continuous read: Home → About → Approach → Investment Focus → Contact → Home. Contact's label is “Return to” / 「トップへ」. No page numbering — the site numbers sections within a page, never the pages themselves.
  • Language routing resolves before first paint: ?lang parameter › stored choice › ja locale default. The shell renders once; a language change swaps text and aria in place and never rebuilds the DOM, so scroll state, drawer state and focus survive the toggle.

4 · Typography

  • DM Sans only, weights 400 and 500, self-hosted. No bold, no italic anywhere. font-display: optional with both faces preloaded, so type never reflows after paint.
  • Japanese resolves to an explicit system CJK stack with line-break: strict; DM Sans carries no Japanese glyphs and is never left to fall through.
  • One global responsive scale — display, page title, h2, h3, statement, body-l, body, caption, label. No page defines its own sizes.
  • Sentence-case headings. Tracked uppercase (0.14em) reserved for short functional English labels only — eyebrows, “Next page”, the location line. Never a tracked uppercase sentence. Japanese removes the transform and drops tracking to ~0.04em.
  • Optical cap indent of −0.02em on display leads. Measures: 60ch body, 38ch lead.
  • Japanese line-heights are raised independently per block, roughly 1.7–1.9.

6 · Imagery

  • Fourteen WebP architectural photographs: four page heroes, two market photographs, three focus subjects at two widths each. Responsive srcset throughout.
  • One grade for the whole collection: saturate(.95) contrast(1.03). No resting blur, no resting zoom — imagery renders sharp and at true scale.
  • Overlay 12% at rest, 7% on hover; the only hover transform is a 1.01 scale. Gradient scrims appear solely where type sits over an image.
  • No stock photography. No AI-generated imagery.

7 · Interaction

  • Motion is secondary. Every static layout is complete without it — the reveal scripts force their final visible state after 1600ms whether or not the observer or the transition ever fires.
  • One clock: 180ms fast · 280ms hover · 260ms section · 380ms panel · 900ms hero exception, on cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Mobile shortens the longer bands.
  • Approved patterns: underline wipe on route terms; market-tab crossfade with a grid-weight shift; sticky process tracker with a scroll-driven spine fill (transform only, never a height change); operating-model hover and focus dimming; focus-page disclosures animating to real content height so Japanese cannot clip; 6px horizontal shift on the next-page title.
  • Every one of the above honours prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard focus is visible on all interactive elements; the drawer traps focus; the market selector uses roving tab order.

CGenuinely unresolved

C1Photograph licences Blocking

All fourteen images in assets/imagery have no purchase record, licence or property release anywhere in the project. This is the one item that can stop a launch outright, and it cannot be resolved from inside the design.

C2Privacy notice — legal review Blocking

Entity name and date are settled (Reiwa Capital K.K.; July 2026, to be re-stamped at sign-off). Still to be confirmed against actual practice: hosting and IP-log retention, cookies or analytics, whether email is processed outside the UK or EEA, which processors and advisers receive personal information, the retention period, and which framework governs (UK GDPR / EU GDPR / APPI). The controller is currently identified by entity name and email only — confirm that publishing no registered address is acceptable in both jurisdictions.

DContradictions found

Between the approved principles and the website as implemented. Ordered by consequence. Nothing here has been changed.

D1Two different business models are on record Resolve first

The project brief in CLAUDE.md describes Reiwa Capital as placing Japanese private capital into UK and Dutch buildings, held in direct ownership — a principal. This freeze brief describes an investor-side firm that coordinates, supports and holds no discretionary authority — an adviser. The website implements the adviser model consistently.

Needed: confirm the investor-side model governs and let me retire the direct-ownership language in CLAUDE.md — or tell me the site is the thing that's wrong. Every other copy question below depends on this answer, so it should be settled first.

D2Home claims property management Copy

The English home lede reads “…the acquisition, ownership and management of European real estate.” The approved positioning says Reiwa Capital must not be presented as a property manager, and the Approach page places property managers among the third-party European Specialists. The Japanese line on the same section already avoids this — it says 現地実行を調整します, coordinates local execution. The English is the outlier.

D4Sentence case is the rule; much of the site is Title Case Decision

Sentence case as approved: “Who we are”, “Market focus”, “Areas of focus”, “Market characteristics”. Title Case against the rule: “Investment Focus”, “Who We Support”, “Investment Mandate”, “Sourcing and Assessment”, “Ownership and Financing”, “Ownership Oversight”, “Asset Quality”, “Investor Authority”, “European Specialists”, and the four Who-we-support terms.

Suggested rule, for approval: page names and navigation labels stay Title Case because they are proper names of destinations; every heading inside a page goes sentence case. That keeps “Investment Focus” as a page title while “Investment mandate” and “Asset quality” become headings.

D5Japanese errors Defect

Listed in full at C3. Recorded here because they contradict the rule that Japanese must read as native, formal Japanese rather than translated text — a wrong character reads worse than a clumsy phrase to the audience these pages are written for.

D6A third ground colour is in use Decision

The palette is approved as cream and sumire. The Who-we-support section on About sits on a pale lavender radial wash (#ECE6F2 fading to cream). It is quiet and it is sumire-derived, but it is a third ground and a gradient. Either ratify it as the site's one colour moment or remove it.

D7The home hero is a full viewport height Decision

min-height: calc(100vh + nav-h), against the rule barring viewport-height sections. A single full-bleed opening image is normal editorial practice and reads as intentional here, unlike the tall purple field that rule was written to kill. It needs an explicit exemption or a fixed height.

D8The hero location line is centred Likely defect

“Tokyo · London · Amsterdam” carries text-align: center while the H1 and the sub-line above it are left-aligned on the same grid, so it floats to the middle of the viewport with nothing to align to. Almost certainly unintended.

D9Decorative brand geometry Decision

A large outline of the Reiwa symbol sits as a background watermark on four sections (home positioning, About identity, Focus assessment, Contact body). The rules bar decorative geometry and anything decorative without purpose. As brand furniture it is defensible; as ornament it is not. Ratify or remove — but it should not sit in the ambiguous middle through a launch audit.

D10The token file offers an unapproved palette Tidy

assets/colors_and_type.css defines sakura pink, plum red, matcha green, indigo blue, gold, slate, a six-series chart palette, a 999px pill radius and five shadow levels. None are used on any live page, and the approved rules bar pills and shadows outright. They read as sanctioned options to anyone opening the file later. Mark them out of scope or delete them.

Recommended order

D1 first — it is the only item that changes what the other copy should say. Then C3 and D2, both of which are wrong on a live page today. C1 and C2 run in parallel and depend on you, not on the design. D4, D6, D7 and D9 are four decisions I can implement in a single pass once you've made them. C5, C6 and D10 are housekeeping and can wait until after the audit.
