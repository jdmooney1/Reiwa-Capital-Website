About

Reiwa Capital advises Japanese investors on the acquisition, structuring and ownership of European real estate.

We act on the investor’s side, from acquisition and financing through ownership and eventual exit.

Our focus spans income, repositioning and hospitality opportunities in London and Amsterdam.

Market characteristics

A transparent, internationally accessible market with deep occupational and investment demand.

Investment focus

Commercial, residential, mixed-use and hospitality assets in established London submarkets.

Reiwa Capital’s role

Identifying opportunities, coordinating acquisition and supporting local ownership workstreams.

Market characteristics

Investment focus

Mixed-use, residential and operational assets within the city’s established central districts.

Reiwa Capital’s role
