Investment Focus

We underwrite every opportunity across the full investment lifecycle: acquisition, financing, ownership and exit.

Every investment is tested against five considerations

Location

Established submarkets with durable demand from occupiers, residents or visitors.

Income

Current and achievable income tested against realistic leasing and operating assumptions.

Asset Quality

Buildings capable of remaining relevant, resilient and competitive for occupiers and future owners.

Execution

A leasing, refurbishment or operating plan that can be delivered in practice, not only modelled.

Exit

Multiple exit routes, an identifiable buyer universe and durable demand at sale.

Areas of Focus

Income

Assets supported by established demand and a visible, defensible income profile.

Asset Types

Offices, prime retail and residential assets supported by established occupier demand.

Investment Considerations

Occupier covenant strength, lease profile, rental sustainability, operational requirements and future reletting prospects.

Repositioning

Assets with identifiable potential to improve occupancy, income and long-term value.

Asset Types

Office and mixed-use buildings with vacancy, underused space or a leasing proposition no longer aligned with occupier demand.

Investment Considerations

Refurbishment scope and cost, planning position, delivery programme, leasing demand, achievable rent and stabilisation period.

Hospitality

Hotels and serviced accommodation where location, operating performance and asset quality support long-term investment value.

Asset Types

Branded and independent hotels, aparthotels, serviced apartments and extended-stay accommodation.

Investment Considerations

Trading performance, operator and brand strength, operating or lease structure, capital requirements, demand drivers and exit liquidity.
