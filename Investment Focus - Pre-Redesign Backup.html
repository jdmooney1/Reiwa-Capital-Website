Investment Focus

Three strategies across London and Amsterdam.

Each opportunity is assessed across acquisition, financing, operation and exit, not simply on purchase price.

Each opportunity is assessed through five filters.

Location Quality

Established submarkets with durable occupier, resident or visitor demand.

Income Profile

Existing or achievable income, on realistic leasing and operating assumptions.

Asset Relevance

Buildings with long‑term use, physical resilience and enduring investor demand.

Execution Requirement

Leasing, refurbishment or operating improvement that local coordination can deliver.

Exit Liquidity

A credible future buyer pool, not one narrow exit route.
