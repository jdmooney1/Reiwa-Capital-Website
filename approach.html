Five Tests, One Investment Case

The Investment Case

Does it meet the investor’s objectives, constraints and ownership requirements?

  • Objectives, hold horizon and reporting requirements
  • Currency, structure and tax constraints

Do demand, liquidity and regulation support the strategy?

  • Occupier demand and competing supply
  • Market depth at this profile
  • Planning, licensing and regulatory direction

Are building, income and capital needs understood and defensible?

  • Building condition, services and compliance
  • Lease structure, covenant and income
  • Capital expenditure during the hold

Is the return acceptable without favourable market movement?

  • Basis against replacement cost and long-run income
  • Exit yield and financing cost sensitivity
  • Return if conditions do not improve

Can the plan be delivered, controlled and realised?

  • Local execution and accountability
  • Decisions reserved to the investor
  • Likely buyer profile at realisation

The Same Building, Held Three Ways

The investor’s objective sets the structure, the hold period and the plan. Each asset is assessed against the objective it is bought for.

Income

Longer leases, low leverage and distributions structured to the investor’s reporting currency.

Depreciation

A high building to land ratio and direct ownership, so the asset’s tax basis is available to the Japanese balance sheet.

Capital Return

Repositioning and re-letting, with the asset positioned for an institutional buyer at realisation.
