01

Investment Mandate

Before opportunities are considered, the investor’s objectives are defined: target returns, hold period, risk tolerance, capital constraints, governance and reporting requirements.

These parameters determine what is pursued and how each opportunity is assessed.

02

Sourcing and Assessment

Opportunities are sourced through local relationships, direct owner contact and adviser networks. Each asset is underwritten on location, income, condition, price, execution requirements and long-term suitability.

The investment case is built at asset level.

03

Ownership and Financing

Ownership structure, financing, tax, foreign exchange and cash-flow requirements are considered together before acquisition.

The recommended structure reflects the investor, the asset and the intended hold period.

04

Acquisition

Reiwa Capital coordinates due diligence, negotiation, documentation and completion across sellers and appointed advisers.

The investor retains approval at each material decision point.

05

Ownership Oversight

Following completion, Reiwa Capital coordinates asset strategy, reporting, leasing, capital works, refinancing and eventual exit against the agreed ownership plan.

This work continues throughout the investment period.

Operating Model

One Partner Across Europe

Reiwa Capital sits between the investor and appointed European specialists, coordinating workstreams, decisions and reporting across the investment lifecycle.

Investor Authority

  • Set investment mandate
  • Commit capital
  • Approve material decisions
  • Decide hold, refinance or exit

  • Translate mandate into execution
  • Source and underwrite
  • Coordinate structuring and acquisition
  • Oversee ownership and reporting

European Specialists

  • Agents and valuers
  • Lenders
  • Legal and tax advisers
  • Technical and project teams
  • Property managers and operating partners

Investment authority remains with the investor. Specialist advice remains with the appointed advisers.
