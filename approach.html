Investment lifecycle

Operating model

Specialists are appointed by, and accountable to, the investor or the ownership vehicle. Specialist advice remains with the relevant appointed advisers; Reiwa Capital coordinates their work against the mandate.

Investment principle

Investment authority remains with the investor.

Reiwa develops the investment case, coordinates execution and oversees the business plan. Material decisions remain subject to investor approval, while specialist advice remains with the relevant appointed advisers.

Investor decides. Reiwa coordinates. Specialists advise.
