Five Tests, One Investment Case

Weighed together, not in order. No single test substitutes for judgement

Mandate Fit
Does the opportunity meet the investor’s objectives, constraints and ownership requirements?
Market Case
Do demand, liquidity and regulation support the proposed strategy?
Asset Fundamentals
Are the building, income and capital requirements understood and defensible?
Pricing and Downside
Does the return remain acceptable without relying on favourable market movement?
Execution and Exit
Can the business plan be delivered, controlled and ultimately realised?
Mandate Fit
  • Objectives, hold horizon and reporting requirements
  • Currency, structure and tax constraints
Market Case
  • Occupier demand and competing supply
  • Depth of the investment market at this profile
  • Planning, licensing and regulatory direction
Asset Fundamentals
  • Building condition, services and compliance
  • Lease structure, covenant and income profile
  • Capital expenditure required during the hold
Pricing and Downside
  • Basis against replacement cost and long-run income
  • Sensitivity to exit yield and financing cost
  • What the case returns if conditions do not improve
Execution and Exit
  • Local execution capability and accountability
  • Decisions reserved to the investor during ownership
  • Likely buyer profile at realisation
