From Mandate
to Ownership.

A five-stage process applied to every mandate, with investor authority retained at each material decision.

Process

Five Stages. One Standard.

Every mandate runs through the same sequence. What changes is the asset, not the discipline. Reiwa Capital coordinates each stage from the investor's side; the investor retains every material decision.

01

Mandate

Objectives, parameters and decision rights.

Before opportunities are considered, the investor's objectives are defined: target returns, hold period, risk tolerance, capital constraints, governance and reporting requirements. These parameters determine what is pursued and how each opportunity is assessed.

Output
An agreed mandate and decision framework.
02

Assess

Sourcing, underwriting and recommendation.

Opportunities are sourced through local relationships, direct owner contact and adviser networks. Each asset is underwritten on location, income, condition, price, execution requirements and long-term suitability, and the investment case is built at asset level rather than assumed from the market.

Output
Asset-level underwriting and a recommendation to proceed or decline.
03

Structure

Ownership, financing and cash flow.

Ownership structure, financing, tax, foreign exchange and cash-flow requirements are considered together before acquisition, with appointed European specialists advising in their own fields. The structure put to the investor reflects the investor, the asset and the intended hold period.

Output
A recommended ownership and financing structure.
04

Acquire

Diligence, negotiation and completion.

Reiwa Capital coordinates due diligence, negotiation, documentation and completion across sellers and appointed advisers, working to the mandate agreed at the outset rather than to the momentum of the transaction.

Output
A completed acquisition and an agreed ownership plan.
05

Oversee

Reporting, business plan and exit.

Following completion, Reiwa Capital coordinates asset strategy, reporting, leasing, capital works, refinancing and eventual exit against the agreed ownership plan. This work continues throughout the investment period.

Output
Continuing reporting and business-plan review through to exit.

Operating model

One Point of Coordination.

Reiwa Capital coordinates the European specialists required to acquire and oversee each investment. Investment authority remains with the investor, and specialist advice remains with the appointed advisers.

Investor

Mandate·Capital·Material approvals·Hold, refinance or exit

Coordination

A single point of coordination between the investor and the market.

Source·Underwrite·Structure·Acquire·Oversee

European specialists
  • Agents and valuers
  • Lenders
  • Legal and tax advisers
  • Technical and project teams
  • Property managers
  • Operating partners

Specialists are appointed by, and accountable to, the investor or the ownership vehicle. Reiwa Capital coordinates their work against the mandate; it does not provide legal, tax, valuation or property-management advice.
