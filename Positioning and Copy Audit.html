Positioning & Copy Audit

Pure copy pass — institutional positioning, verb accuracy, EN/JA correspondence — across all live copy, 31 July 2026. No website changes made. Cross-references the prior Design & Content Freeze and Inventory & Consistency Audit where a finding overlaps, but every recommended wording below is new.

1Required accuracy corrections

Copy that states or implies something the rest of the site itself contradicts.

R1RequiredHome claims "management" of the real estate — the site's own Operating Model says otherwise

Page & section

Home → Positioning statement

Current wording

"Reiwa Capital supports overseas investors through the acquisition, ownership and management of European real estate, with a focus on London and Amsterdam."

Problem

Approach's own Operating Model lists "Property managers and operating partners" under third-party European Specialists — the site elsewhere is careful that Reiwa coordinates, not manages. This is the one place that contradicts it. Home's own Japanese sibling already avoids the word (現地実行を調整します — "coordinates local execution"); the English is the outlier, not the house style.

Risk category

Property-manager confusion — regulatory/credibility exposure

Recommended wording

"...the acquisition, ownership and oversight of European real estate..."

Reason for change

"Oversight" isn't invented for this fix — it's the exact word Approach already uses for the same idea (stage 5, "Ownership Oversight" / 保有支援). Reusing it ties Home to a term the site has already approved, rather than adding new language.

EN/JA implications

JA needs no change — it already avoids the claim. Only the EN string moves.

R2RequiredHome's own "Manage" route heading carries the same risk, in miniature

Page & section

Home → role routes (Acquire / Structure / Manage)

Current wording

Heading: "Manage" / 管理 — supporting line: "Monitor performance, coordinate reporting and support key ownership decisions through to exit."

Problem

The supporting sentence is fine — pure coordination language. But a heading is read before its body copy, and "Manage" in isolation, next to "Acquire" and "Structure," reads as a third thing Reiwa does to the property — exactly the confusion the rules warn against — even though the sentence beneath it doesn't claim that.

Risk category

Property-manager confusion — heading-level, not body-level

Recommended wording

"Oversee" / 監督

Reason for change

Approach's process tracker already uses precisely this pair for the identical stage (data-en="Oversight" data-ja="監督"). Same reasoning as R1: an existing, approved term, not a new one.

EN/JA implications

Both strings change; the pairing stays parallel.

R3RequiredOperating Model intro uses the one verb already flagged and removed elsewhere

Page & section

Approach → Operating Model, intro paragraph

Current wording

"...Reiwa Capital aligns the local expertise and workstreams required to acquire and own the asset."

Problem

A prior copy pass on this site specifically removed "align" from Home's "Structure" route because it isn't in the approved verb set (advises / identifies / assesses / coordinates / supports / reviews) and can read as Reiwa acting on its own authority. This exact word has resurfaced in the Operating Model rebuild. Its own Japanese sibling already uses the correct verb: 投資家側の立場から一貫して調整します — coordinates. EN and JA now disagree on Reiwa's core verb, in the section that exists specifically to define Reiwa's role.

Risk category

Verb-set regression + EN/JA mismatch on a role-defining sentence

Recommended wording

"...Reiwa Capital coordinates the local expertise and workstreams required to acquire and own the asset."

Reason for change

Restores the approved verb and matches the JA original, which was correct all along.

EN/JA implications

JA unchanged; EN brought into line with it.

R4RequiredA tax/legal disclaimer exists in Japanese but has no English equivalent

Page & section

Approach → Process, stage 03 "Ownership and Financing"

Current wording

EN: "Ownership, financing, tax, foreign exchange and reporting requirements are considered together before an acquisition proceeds."
JA: 保有形態、資金調達、税務、為替、報告の要件を、取得に先立ち一体的に検討します。税務・法務等の専門的助言は、各分野の専門家と連携して確保します。

Problem

The Japanese carries a second sentence with no English counterpart: specialist tax and legal advice is secured through coordination with experts in each field. This is precisely the disclaimer the audit brief asks about — that Reiwa does not provide tax/legal advice itself — and today only the Japanese reader receives it at this point in the process. An English diligence reader gets less protective disclosure than a Japanese one on the same page.

Risk category

Regulated-advice implication — EN/JA content parity gap

Recommended wording

Extend the EN stage-sup line: "The recommended structure reflects the investor, the asset and the intended hold period, with specialist tax and legal advice secured through appointed advisers."

Reason for change

Folds the missing disclaimer into the existing second sentence rather than adding a new one — keeps the stage's length and rhythm matched to the other four.

EN/JA implications

JA needs no change; it was already correct. Confirm whether the divergence was deliberate before applying — if intentional, the EN gap should still be closed some other way, since the disclaimer matters in both languages.

2Positioning improvements

Not wrong, but not saying the same thing twice — or saying it with less precision than the site does elsewhere.

P1PositioningThree different English one-line positioning statements are simultaneously live

Page & section

Home (hero sub-line), About (page sub), every <title>/OG tag

Current wording

About: "Cross-border real estate investment in Europe" (the approved line) — Home: "Cross-Border Real Estate Investment" (title case, "in Europe" dropped) — meta: "European Real Estate, Structured for Overseas Capital" (a third, longer line)

Problem

A reader comparing pages, or a shared link's preview card, meets three non-matching descriptions of the same firm in the same short visit.

Risk category

Positioning-statement duplication/drift

Recommended wording

Reuse the approved line verbatim on Home: "Cross-border real estate investment in Europe." Nothing about the hero's line length requires the shortened variant — it's a single short line either way. Leave metadata as its own, longer SEO-oriented line if that split is intentional; if not, align it too.

Reason for change

One statement, one wording, repeated exactly — not paraphrased — is what makes a positioning line read as considered rather than incidental.

EN/JA implications

JA is unaffected — its own single positioning line (日本の投資家による欧州不動産投資を支援) is already used consistently everywhere it appears.

P2PositioningLondon's market description reads more promotional than Amsterdam's parallel panel

Page & section

About → Market focus → London, "Market characteristics"

Current wording

"One of Europe's deepest and most transparent real estate markets, supported by global occupier demand, transaction liquidity and specialist expertise."

Compare Amsterdam's parallel line, which carries no comparable superlative: "A supply-constrained European market shaped by protected historic stock, limited development capacity and strong demand for well-located assets."

Problem

The claim is hedged ("one of," not "the") and defensible on the merits, so this isn't inaccurate — but the two panels, sitting one tab apart, read in noticeably different registers: London reaches for superlatives, Amsterdam stays structural and factual.

Risk category

Tone inconsistency between parallel sections — low severity

Recommended wording

"A deep, transparent real estate market, supported by global occupier demand, transaction liquidity and specialist expertise."

Reason for change

Keeps the substantive claim, drops the comparative framing, matches Amsterdam's descriptive register.

EN/JA implications

JA's London line (高い透明性と取引流動性を備え、世界の投資家とテナントが集まる、欧州有数の不動産市場です) carries a softer version of the same idea already — 欧州有数 ("among Europe's foremost") is milder than "deepest and most transparent." Bringing EN down slightly moves it closer to its own JA sibling, not further away.

P3Positioning"Coordinates... exit" could read as Reiwa executing the sale itself

Page & section

Approach → Process, stage 05 "Ownership Oversight"

Current wording

"...coordinates reporting, business planning, leasing, capital works, refinancing and eventual exit against the agreed ownership plan."

Problem

Coordinating an exit is a defensible, accurate description of the role — but standing alone at the end of a list, "exit" reads slightly closer to executing a sale than the site's careful coordinating/advising framing elsewhere. Minor.

Risk category

Coordinate-vs-execute ambiguity — low severity

Recommended wording

"...coordinates reporting, business planning, leasing, capital works, refinancing and eventual exit alongside the investor's advisers, against the agreed ownership plan."

Reason for change

Names the advisers doing the transaction work, the same pattern the Operating Model already uses.

EN/JA implications

Apply the same qualifier to JA (…売却までを投資家側で支援します already says "supports on the investor's side," which is close — confirm whether it needs the explicit adviser mention too).
