Investment Focus

Selected areas of focus across London and Amsterdam.

Purchase price is only one part of the investment case. Each opportunity is assessed across acquisition, financing, operation and exit.

Five considerations shape every assessment.

Location

Established submarkets with durable demand from occupiers, residents or visitors.

Income

Current and achievable income, tested against realistic leasing and operating assumptions.

Asset quality

Buildings capable of remaining useful, resilient and attractive to occupiers and future owners.

Execution

A defined route to improvement through leasing, refurbishment or operations.

Exit

A credible pool of future buyers, rather than dependence on a single exit route.
