# Reiwa Capital — Design Brief

Tokyo-based cross-border real estate platform placing Japanese private capital into
UK and Dutch buildings, held in **direct ownership**. Direct ownership sits with the
investor — not via a pooled fund vehicle. Reiwa Capital is the investor-side adviser and
coordinator: it holds nothing itself, and is never presented as principal, fund manager or
property manager. Confirmed as the resolved reading; the site's advisory/coordination
model is correct as built. The site is a calm, institutional,
design-led marketing site. Restraint is the point: it should feel like a **private office,
not a startup**. Avoid anything that reads as templated or AI-generated.

## Positioning (one line)
European real estate, structured for overseas capital — owned directly, overseen on the ground.
EN and JA intentionally diverge here: EN says "overseas capital"; JA addresses Japanese investors
directly (日本の投資家). Not a mismatch — do not force these to match verbatim.

## Audience
Japanese family offices, HNW investors, and corporates. Senior, discreet, diligence-minded.
Many read Japanese first. Must feel trustworthy to someone who will reference-check everything.

## Voice & copy rules
- Short, formal, zero hype. No sales energy, no adjectives doing persuasion.
- If the layout already shows it, the copy must not say it. Cut every redundant clause.
- Full stops over commas for rhythm. Sparing use of em-dashes only.
- Fully bilingual EN / JA via a toggle. Japanese must be native, formal, terse — not translated-sounding.
- Never name a deal, client, or figure. Confidentiality is a feature: "by introduction",
  "a small number of investors".
