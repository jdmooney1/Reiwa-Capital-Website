Copy Refinement & Asset-Rights Register

Controlled pass — live production files only (the six root pages + shared shell.js/craft.js/editorial.css). No stylistic rewriting; changes limited to genuine repetition, ambiguity, unsupported claims and one verb-consistency tweak.

Method & finding

Checked all six live pages against the banned/watch-listed term list ("cross-border", "institutional", "disciplined", "patient capital", "long-term ownership", "local expertise", "value creation"), the inflated-language list ("unparalleled", "best-in-class", "unique access", "market-leading"), and every claim of Reiwa Capital's role against the approved verb set (advises / identifies / assesses / coordinates / supports / reviews).

Result: the site is already close to clean — a prior rewrite pass already removed "disciplined", "patient capital", "institutional" and "value creation" from every live page (they survive only in old prototype/export snapshots under audit/, dist/ and export/, which are not served). Three genuine, narrow issues remained, corrected below. No inflated-language, guaranteed-return, discretionary-authority or unregistered-manager claims were found anywhere live.

Positioning & verb-accuracy audit

Approach's Responsibilities ledger and five-stage process already map cleanly onto the approved framework — "the investor retains authority", "Reiwa Capital coordinates execution", explicit investor approval at each material decision point — and needed no change. No page anywhere claims Reiwa Capital holds investor capital, exercises discretionary investment authority, guarantees a return, provides regulated tax/legal/financial advice directly, owns an asset, or acts as an authorised fund or asset manager. Privacy Policy's security language is correctly hedged ("no method... can be guaranteed to be completely secure") rather than overpromising.

Entity status

The site remains correctly entity-neutral: no registration number, registered address, regulatory permission, founding date or named personnel appear anywhere, live or in the schema.org markup (which carries only name, URL, logo and the LinkedIn/email contact points). Privacy Policy's [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS OR JURISDICTION, IF REQUIRED] and [VERIFIED DATE] remain exactly as bracketed, visibly-flagged placeholders — left untouched here, since inventing entity detail to make them "look finished" would be worse than the honest placeholder. These still require the operator to supply real information before launch.

Legal content check

  • Copyright notice — "© 2026 Reiwa Capital", present on every page via the shared footer. Minimal format (no "All rights reserved" suffix) is legally sufficient and consistent with the site's restraint; the name used should match whatever legal entity is eventually confirmed for the Privacy Policy.
  • Privacy Policy completeness — unchanged in this pass; still carries the three bracketed placeholders and its own in-source review comment listing exactly what needs confirming (hosting/log retention, cookie/analytics use, cross-border processing, applicable framework). Not duplicated here — see the Pre-Launch Audit for the full list.
  • External links — the one external link (LinkedIn) correctly uses target="_blank" rel="noopener".
  • Analytics, forms, cookies, embeds — none exist on the site beyond a single localStorage write for language preference, which is already disclosed in Privacy §01 and correctly described as not used for advertising or tracking. No additional disclosure is currently triggered, and no cookie banner is needed while this remains true.
  • Disclaimer tone — Privacy Policy already presents rights and obligations as conditional on applicable law ("depending on the applicable law...", "where applicable...") rather than asserting settled legal fact. No change needed.
