Focus Markets

Two markets, and within each a deliberately narrow focus.

West End City of London Midtown
  • Core focus
  • Selective focus

Focus areas are indicative and boundaries are approximate.

Market Characteristics

Deep capital markets, a broad occupier base and a mature financing and advisory network. Performance varies materially by street, building quality, tenure and lease profile.

Why We Focus Here

Breadth of occupier demand supports rental income, and depth of buyers supports the exit. The variation from asset to asset is what rewards investor-side selection.

What We Look For

  • Prime central or established commercial locations with evidenced occupier demand
  • Durable income, or a specific and controllable route to improvement
  • Realistic capital requirements and more than one credible exit route
Canal Belt & Jordaan Oud-Zuid Zuidas
  • Core focus
  • Selective focus

Focus areas are indicative and boundaries are approximate.

Market Characteristics

Compact and supply constrained, shaped by strict planning and sustainability requirements. High-quality property in established central and business locations remains scarce.

Why We Focus Here

Scarcity and durable occupier demand suit long holding periods. Differences in title, permitted use and condition between similar buildings reward diligence before commitment.

What We Look For

  • Established central and business locations with clear occupational demand
  • Clear permitted use and manageable technical and sustainability requirements
  • Durable income, or a credible route through leasing, repositioning or operational improvement
