Who we are

Reiwa Capital supports overseas investors in the acquisition, structuring and ownership of European real estate.

Acting on the investor side, we coordinate local execution from initial assessment and financing through ownership and exit.

Market focus

Our focus is deliberately concentrated on London and Amsterdam. Depth in two markets allows us to build the local relationships and asset-level knowledge needed to assess opportunities and support ownership.

Market characteristics

One of Europe’s deepest and most transparent real estate markets, supported by global occupier demand, transaction liquidity and specialist expertise.

Investment focus

Income-producing and repositioning opportunities across commercial, residential, mixed-use and hospitality assets in established London submarkets.

Reiwa Capital’s role

We source and assess opportunities, coordinate acquisition and financing, and support ownership decisions through to exit.

Market characteristics

A supply-constrained European market shaped by protected historic stock, limited development capacity and strong demand for well-located assets.

Investment focus

Income-producing and repositioning opportunities across mixed-use, residential and hospitality assets in Amsterdam’s established central districts.

Reiwa Capital’s role
