Why Reiwa exists

Cross-border ownership needs continuity.

Acquiring an overseas asset is only one part of the investment. Investor objectives need to remain clear through underwriting, execution and ownership, even as local advisers, counterparties and market conditions change. Reiwa Capital is built to provide that continuity from the investor’s side.

01

Investor Mandate

Objectives, parameters and decision requirements.

We begin with what the investor is trying to achieve, how capital should be deployed and what conditions need to be satisfied.

02

Local Execution

Sourcing, diligence, financing and acquisition.

Each opportunity is assessed and coordinated against the original mandate rather than pursued simply because it is available.

03

Ownership Oversight

Business planning, reporting and key ownership decisions.

After acquisition, the same investor perspective continues through performance monitoring, strategic decisions and eventual exit.

Focus markets

Two Markets. Deeper Focus.

Reiwa Capital concentrates initially on London and Amsterdam. The objective is not broad geographic coverage. It is to build repeatable judgement within a smaller number of markets and submarkets, supported by local relationships, detailed underwriting and continued ownership oversight.
