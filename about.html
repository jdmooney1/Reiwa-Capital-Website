Market Characteristics

Deep capital markets and mature financing and advisory networks. Central London comprises distinct submarkets, each assessed on its own merits.

Why We Focus Here

Each opportunity is assessed at submarket and asset level, with the business plan tested against occupier demand, liquidity and exit conditions. This is done before an investor commits capital.

What We Look For

  • Prime central locations with evidenced demand
  • Durable income, or a controllable route to improvement
  • Realistic capital needs and more than one exit route

Market Characteristics

Compact and supply-constrained, with strict planning and sustainability requirements. The market has established occupier demand and includes institutional buyers.

Why We Focus Here

Strict rules make permitted use and technical condition decisive. Appointed specialists review both before purchase; Reiwa then monitors both, and any capital required, through ownership.

What We Look For

  • Established central locations with clear demand
  • Clear permitted use, manageable technical requirements
  • Durable income, or a route through leasing or repositioning
