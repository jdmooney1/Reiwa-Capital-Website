Focus Markets

Two markets, and within each a deliberately narrow focus.

London

West End City of London Midtown
  • Core
  • Selective

Market Characteristics

Deep capital markets and mature financing and advisory networks. Central London comprises distinct submarkets. We assess each opportunity at the level of location, building quality and occupier demand.

Why We Focus Here

The decision rests on the asset and its business plan more than on the market. Reiwa assesses both, and the exit route, before an investor commits.

What We Look For

  • Prime central locations with evidenced demand
  • Durable income, or a controllable route to improvement
  • Realistic capital needs and more than one exit route

Amsterdam

Canal Belt Oud-Zuid Zuidas
  • Core
  • Selective

Market Characteristics

Compact and supply constrained, with strict planning and sustainability requirements. The market has established occupier demand and includes institutional buyers.

Why We Focus Here

Strict rules make permitted use and technical condition decisive. Appointed specialists review both before purchase, and Reiwa monitors them, and the capital they may require, through ownership.

What We Look For

  • Established central locations with clear demand
  • Clear permitted use, manageable technical requirements
  • Durable income, or a route through leasing or repositioning
