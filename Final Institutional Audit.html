Final Institutional Audit

Full-journey review of the live site (all six pages, shell.js, i18n.js, editorial.css, brand.css, craft.css, colors_and_type.css) against the Design & Content Freeze, the prior Pre-Launch Audit, CLAUDE.md and the ten review stages requested. Cross-checked against ~25 prior working documents in audit/ so settled items aren't re-litigated. No changes have been made — this is the report only.

Progress since the last full pass is real: the Japanese character errors, the Japanese/Overseas contradiction, the fixed-pixel market-panel bug, the disclosure-label ambiguity, Home's duplicate role links and the no-JS shell fallback are all confirmed fixed. Seven items below are serious enough to resolve before this goes in front of an investor or bank, and three of those need a decision from you, not a build fix.

Critical — 7

Materially damages positioning, credibility, usability or accuracy.

CriticalBusiness model contradiction is still openPositioning · decision needed

Page
Governs all — CLAUDE.md vs. the live site
Issue
CLAUDE.md still describes Reiwa Capital as placing capital into buildings held in direct ownership — a principal. Every live page (Operating Model, the five-stage process, About) implements an investor-side adviser that holds no discretionary authority and coordinates specialists. This was flagged in the Design & Content Freeze and has not been resolved.
Why it matters
It's the answer to "who is Reiwa Capital and what does it do." Every copy judgment below — "management" vs. "oversight," whether a claim sounds like a fund manager — depends on which model is real.
Action
Confirm the investor-side model is the real one and let CLAUDE.md be corrected to match, or say the site is wrong. Nothing else should be finalised until this is answered.
