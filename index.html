Two Markets

London and Amsterdam, with a deliberately narrow focus.

Explore Focus Markets→

London

Deep capital markets, broad occupier demand and mature financing networks. Reiwa focuses on the asset, its business plan and the credibility of the exit.

  • Breadth of occupier demand
  • Highly differentiated micro-markets
  • Depth of buyers at exit

Amsterdam

A compact, supply-constrained market shaped by strict planning and sustainability requirements. Permitted use, technical condition and ownership capital needs are central to assessment.

  • Compact and supply-constrained
  • Established occupier demand
  • Includes institutional buyers
